# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Criticize code, not people.

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming to people of any background or experience level.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully, and give it kindly.
- Remember that someone filing a confusing bug report is trying to help.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report problems by opening a private
[security advisory](https://github.com/shihabshahrier/Create-Your-Own-Vpn-Server/security/advisories/new)
or contacting the maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, or contributors that violate
this policy.
